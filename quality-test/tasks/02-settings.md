# Task 2: settings screen for a podcast app

Design the Settings screen of a podcast app called Fathom for iPhone, in SwiftUI. Deployment target iOS 27.0. Platform check: `#if os(iOS)`. Top-level view name: `FathomSettingsScreen`.

Settings to include:

- Account: signed-in name and email, manage subscription.
- Playback: playback speed, skip forward interval, skip back interval, continuous play, trim silence, default sleep timer.
- Downloads: auto-download new episodes, download over cellular, remove played episodes, storage used with a way to manage it.
- Notifications: new episodes, recommendations.
- Appearance: app theme (system, light, dark), app icon choice.
- Privacy: share analytics.
- About: version, acknowledgements, rate the app, contact support.

Give every row an appropriate SF Symbol. Every symbol must exist and be available on the deployment target. Follow Apple's design guidance for settings, lists, toggles, typography and color.
