# Task 1: recipes app for iPhone Duo

Design the main screen of a recipes app called Larder for iPhone Duo, in SwiftUI. Deployment target iOS 27.1. Platform check: `#if os(iOS)`. Top-level view name: `LarderDuoScreen`.

The app has four top-level destinations: Recipes, Meal Plan, Shopping List and Settings. Build the Recipes destination fully and leave the other three as simple placeholders.

Recipes shows a list of recipes and, for the selected recipe, a photo placeholder, its ingredients and its steps. Actions on a recipe: favorite, share, start cooking, add ingredients to the shopping list, edit, delete.

The screen must work well on the outer display when the device is closed, on the inner display when it is open in portrait and in landscape, and when the device is partially folded like a book. Follow Apple's design guidance for iPhone Duo.
